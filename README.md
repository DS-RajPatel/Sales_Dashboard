📊 Sales Dashboard
Author: Raj Patel
Project: Sales and Profit Analysis Dashboard

📝 Overview
This project presents an interactive sales dashboard designed to analyze key business metrics such as sales, profit, quantity, and discount performance across multiple dimensions. The dashboard helps visualize and explore how different product categories, customer segments, regions, payment modes, and time periods contribute to overall business outcomes.

🔍 Key Features
✅ Interactive quarter selection (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)
✅ Sum of Profit by Sub-Category (e.g., Printers, Phones, Accessories)
✅ Quantity breakdown by Payment Mode
✅ Total metrics (Quantity, Profit, Amount)
✅ Quantity and Amount by Product Category
✅ Amount by Customer Name (top performers)
✅ Monthly profit trends

💡 Insights
Top Sub-Categories: Printers lead in profit, followed by Phones and Accessories.

Top Customers: Yogesh and Vini are the highest contributors by sales amount.

Quarterly Trends: Q1 shows strong profits, with January peaking at 9.7K.

Payment Mode: Largest share of transactions comes from a single payment mode (43.58%).
